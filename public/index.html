Loose Change does not give financial advice. It adds up what your statement already says and shows you where to look.

How do I know nothing is uploaded?

  • The page forbids it. It carries a rule (a Content Security Policy) that tells your browser to refuse every connection except to the address it was loaded from. You can read it at the top of the page source.
  • The model is a file, not a service. A 23 MB language model is downloaded once with the page and runs in your browser. There is no AI company on the other end.
  • Try it offline. Load the page, switch off your Wi-Fi, then drop in a statement. It still works.
  • Nothing is kept. Your statement lives in this tab's memory and is gone when you close it. Only the corrections you teach it are saved, in this browser, and “Start over” can forget those too.
